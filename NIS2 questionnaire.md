# NIS2 Questionnaire

- Do you have an Information Security Policy in place? (true/false)
- Is a CISO or equivalent security officer appointed? (true/false)
- Are information security roles and responsibilities clearly defined? (true/false)
- Are security policies reviewed and updated at least annually? (true/false)
- Are employees required to undergo regular security awareness training? (true/false)
- Is Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) implemented for all remote access? (true/false)
- Are all sensitive data encrypted at rest and in transit? (true/false)
- Do you have endpoint protection installed and managed on all devices? (true/false)
- Is internal network segmentation implemented to isolate critical systems? (true/false)
- Do you use a Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) system? (true/false)
- Are logs retained and monitored for anomalous activities? (true/false)
- Are privileged accounts controlled and monitored? (true/false)
- Is user access reviewed regularly? (true/false)
- Are onboarding and offboarding processes clearly defined and documented? (true/false)
- Do you maintain an incident response plan? (true/false)
- Have you tested your incident response plan in the past 12 months? (true/false)
- Are you able to notify clients of a breach within 24 hours? (true/false)
- Have you experienced a cybersecurity breach in the last 12 months? (true/false)
- Is there a documented and tested BCP/DR plan? (true/false)
- Are backups encrypted, tested, and stored offline? (true/false)
- Are recovery objectives (RTO/RPO) defined and met? (true/false)
- Do you assess the cybersecurity posture of your own vendors/subcontractors? (true/false)
- Are third-party risks included in your risk register? (true/false)
- Do you contractually require your vendors to meet cybersecurity standards? (true/false)
- Do you hold any cybersecurity certifications (e.g., ISO 27001, SOC 2)? (true/false)
- Are you compliant with relevant regulations (e.g., GDPR, NIS2, DORA)? (true/false)
- If you answered YES to experiencing a cybersecurity breach in the past 12 months, please describe. (free text)
- List any cybersecurity certifications you hold. (free text)
- List all applicable regulations your company is compliant with. (free text)
- Are systems regularly patched within a defined SLA? (true/false)
- Is there 24/7 security monitoring (internal or external)? (true/false)
